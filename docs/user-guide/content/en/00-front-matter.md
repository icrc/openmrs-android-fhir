# About this guide {#about .unnumbered}

This guide helps you use the **OpenMRS tablet app** to register patients, record what you do for
them and keep your work safe, even when there is no internet.

It is short on purpose. It does not describe every button: the **?** button on each screen starts a
tour for that. It explains how to get things done, and warns you about the things that confuse people
most. If you read only one chapter, read [Things that confuse people](#things-to-know).

## Who this guide is for {#audience .unnumbered}

You use the app in the field or in a clinic. You know how to use a tablet, but you do not need to
know anything about software.

## How to read it {#how-to-read .unnumbered}

- Words in **bold** are the words you see on the screen, in your language.
- Numbers on the pictures match the numbers in the steps.
- **Tip** gives a faster way. **Warning** tells you about something that could lose your work or
  cause a mistake.
- All names, numbers and places in the pictures are made up. They are not real people.

> **Note:** the app can look a little different in your organisation. Your organisation chooses
> which sign-in options, forms and patient lists you see.

## What is new in this edition {#whats-new .unnumbered}

This edition is shorter and written as tips. It adds the new ways of working with patient lists
(create a list, add patients to it, start a group session from it) and the **Action needed** message
on the sync page.
