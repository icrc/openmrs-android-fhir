# About this guide {#about .unnumbered}

This guide explains how to use the **OpenMRS tablet app** to register patients, record what you
do for them and keep your work safe, even when there is no internet.

## Who this guide is for {#audience .unnumbered}

You use the app in the field or in a clinic. You know how to use a tablet, but you do not need to
know anything about software. Each step is short and does one thing.

## How to read it {#how-to-read .unnumbered}

- Words in **bold** are the words you see on the screen. They are written exactly as the app shows
  them, in your language.
- Numbers on the pictures match the numbers in the steps.
- A box that starts with **Note** gives extra information. **Tip** gives a faster way. **Warning**
  tells you about something that could lose your work or cause a mistake.
- All names, numbers and places in the pictures are made up. They are not real people.

> **Note:** The app can look a little different in your organisation. Your organisation chooses
> which sign-in options, forms and lists you see. This guide shows a typical set-up.

## What is new in this edition {#whats-new .unnumbered}

This is the first edition of the guide. It covers signing in, patients, forms, visits, group
sessions, synchronising, import and export, settings and security.
