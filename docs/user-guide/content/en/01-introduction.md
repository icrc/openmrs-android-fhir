# Introduction {#introduction}

## What the app does {#what-it-does}

The app helps you look after patients. You can:

- register a new patient and find patients you already know;
- record a visit or fill in a form for a patient (an **encounter**);
- fill in one form for several patients at once (a **group or family session**);
- send your work to the central server, and bring the latest data back (**sync**).

The app keeps a copy of your patients **on the tablet**. This means you can work without internet.
When you are connected, the app sends what you recorded.

## Key ideas in plain words {#key-ideas}

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Patient | A person you care for. The app stores their name, age, contact details and what you recorded. |
| Patient list | A group of patients, for example "Morning clinic". You choose which lists to keep on the tablet. |
| Location | The place where you work. What you record is saved under this place. |
| Form | A set of questions you answer for a patient, for example a screening scale. |
| Encounter | One time you filled in a form for a patient. |
| Visit | A period of care that can hold several encounters. Your organisation decides if you manage visits yourself. |
| Sync | Sending your work to the server and receiving the latest data. |

## What you need {#what-you-need}

- A tablet with the app installed.
- A user name and password (or a single sign-on account) from your organisation.
- An internet connection **the first time**, to download your patients. After that you can work offline.

## Using the app on a tablet {#tablet-tips}

- The app works upright (portrait) and turns with the tablet.
- The screens are the same as on a phone, only larger. Lists are wider and easier to read.
- Hold the tablet with both hands when you type. The keyboard covers the lower half of the screen.
- The **?** button at the top right of most screens starts a short tour of that screen.
