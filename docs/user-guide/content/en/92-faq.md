# Frequently asked questions {#faq}

## Can I work without internet? {#faq-offline}

Yes. You can register patients and record forms offline. The first download needs a connection. Everything you record is sent at the next sync.

## How do I know that my work was sent? {#faq-sent}

The home screen says {{home_summary_uploaded}}, and the sync page shows nothing in the {{sync_tab_pending}} list.

## What happens if I log out? {#faq-logout}

By default your data stays on the tablet, and the next person to sign in finds it. The question at log out tells you what happens.

## Can another user see my patients? {#faq-other-users}

Not unless you allow it in {{settings}}. Each user sees only the patients they registered or downloaded.

## I registered the same patient twice. What now? {#faq-duplicate}

Tell your organisation, who can merge them on the server. To avoid it, search before you register, and use {{identify_later_action}} when you cannot reach the server.

## Why do I not see the same forms as a colleague? {#faq-forms}

The forms and their sections can be set by your organisation, and each person can make their own sections in {{settings}}.

## How do I change the language? {#faq-language}

Open {{settings}} and choose a language at the top, or tap the settings icon on the login page.

## Is my data safe on the tablet? {#faq-safe}

The app can lock itself with your fingerprint, face or screen lock (see [Security, users and your data](#lock-screen)). Keep your tablet's own screen lock on.
